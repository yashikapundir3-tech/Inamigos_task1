About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. The foundation works with the vision of creating a compassionate and socially responsible community.

Through its initiatives, the foundation focuses on food and clothing support, education for underprivileged children, animal welfare, women empowerment, environmental conservation and skill development.

Its purpose is not only to provide help, but also to inspire people to participate in social change through volunteering, awareness campaigns and community service.
